## ✨ Features

🧿 **ESP & Visual**

* **Player ESP** – Toggle main overlay to see all players
* **Skeletons** – Display detailed player skeletal structures
* **Player Head & Direction** – View head positions and facing directions
* **Player Info** – Show player names and distance indicators
* **Snaplines** – Custom lines pointing to targets with body part selection
* **Hide Teammates** – Filter out friendly players and Hunters

🎯 **Combat**

* **Aimbot** – Master precision aim assist system
* **Visibility Check** – Ensure targets are visible before locking
* **Aim Bone** - Target specific zones such as Head, Neck, or Body
* **FOV Circle** – Adjustable field of view aiming radius
* **Smoothing** – Customize aim movement transitions
* **Aim Key** – Fully customizable activation key

⚡ **Movement & Host Options**

* **Teleport (HOST ONLY)** – Instantly change your location
* **Speed Hack (HOST ONLY)** – Increase movement speed multiplier
* **Spinbot** – Customizable player rotation effects

🛡️ **Protection & Gameplay**

* **God Mode** – Complete invincibility
* **Auto Paint** – Automatic paint spraying and instant camouflage
* **Anti-Kick** – Protection against lobby disconnects
* **Anti-Buried** – Prevent getting stuck in terrain or objects

🛠️ **Utility**

* **No Gun Cooldown** – Fire weapons continuously without delay
* **FOV Changer** – Adjust custom field of view values
* **Chat Spam** – Send automated messages with customizable intervals
* **Menu Toggle** – Press INSERT or F1 to open overlay

## ⚙️ Installation

1. Click the download button above

3. Done!

## 📑 How to Use

1. Launch the MecchaAutoPainter.exe
2. Start MECCHA CHAMELEON
3. Press **INSERT** to open the menu
4. Toggle features you want
5. Play and dominate!
